Do you also feel cheated?

Since the agent boom, I see more and more teams adopting AI and working harder and producing more, but at the cost of AI babysitting and high, sometimes unrealistic expectations from management.

Instead of full delegation, we are literally working harder, not smarter.

We are trying to fix it for our customers using a reliable, vendor-agnostic async agent tasks platform. We are still not there.

Curious if you feel the same.
