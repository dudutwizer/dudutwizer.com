Imagine someone comes to your house, replaces the doors, locks, lights, and appliances, then says:

"Good news, your house is now AI-Native."

But from now on, every time you want to open a door, turn on a light, or use the kitchen, you need to ask them for permission and pay them.

Sounds absurd.

But this is exactly what happens when FDEs and SAs build "AI solutions" inside your company.

They don't give you an AI stack. They give you dependency.

The future belongs to companies that own their AI stack, stay vendor-agnostic, and can run agents across any system.

Until recently, that required 20-30 platform engineers, 12-24 months, and endless maintenance. Now it's possible with xpander. :)
