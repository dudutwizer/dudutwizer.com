AI as the micromanager?

Micromanagement used to be a bad thing. Maybe it still is.

But in a call about how they're using xpander, we discovered that their call-center agents now get notes cleaned, organized, and ready for the next call in under 20 seconds. Before, it took around five minutes.

Not humans managing AI. AI managing the human workflow.

On the personal-agent side, agents are turning email, Slack, Jira, GitHub, and Notion mess into simple daily checkboxes in your favorite to-do app (mine is Things 3).

BTW, this is becoming one of the leading Codex / OpenClaw use cases here in the Valley.

It might be the most useful AI layer: watching the mess, extracting the work, and telling you what needs to happen next.
