*(Originally written in Hebrew; translated to English.)*

We've gotten comments that we're "not the only ones" building infrastructure for agents. And that's true.

There are quite a few startups building agents today. Some even call themselves Agent Infrastructure.

But here's exactly the difference.

An external, vertical agent that works like an outside vendor or a smart SaaS is not an internal agent. And a platform that swaps out your libraries, wraps your code, or takes over the runtime is not really infrastructure.

We build internal agent infrastructure: agents that run inside the organization's own environment, with its permissions, its interfaces, and its constraints. Agents that can run for hours, days, and weeks without anyone babysitting them.

This wasn't possible until recently. Not because of the models, but because of the infrastructure, and that's what we're fixing.

Instead of replacing agentic development libraries or hiding your code, we built a new infrastructure layer that doesn't touch the core. The developer keeps full control of the code at every moment. And for those who don't code, we created library-based templates that generate real agents without writing a single line (and the moment we did that, our customers built things that surprised even us).

If there's anyone else building internal infrastructure for long-running agents, the kind that doesn't replace libraries, doesn't change your code, and works even in environments disconnected from the cloud, we'd genuinely love to connect. :)

And in the meantime, we're hiring. Tel Aviv and San Francisco. 🚀
