Excited to announce the xpander.ai and NVIDIA NIM product integration, bringing AI one step closer to practical AI Agents in enterprises.

Our platform powers a Text-to-Action runtime, enabling AI agents to not just respond but take reliable actions and get live data by accessing 1st-party and 3rd-party APIs using xpander's Agentic Interfaces.

With NVIDIA NIM and xpander.ai, businesses can automatically generate graph-driven agents, turning text inputs into tangible results. This collaboration makes integrating AI with existing systems seamless, empowering enterprises to stay ahead in the AI-driven landscape.
