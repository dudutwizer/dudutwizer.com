**Most AI agents are just fancy chatbots.**

Wrapped in hype. Disconnected from execution.

Bessemer Venture Partners published their AI Agent Autonomy Scale - a strong framework for measuring maturity across agentic systems. It's a helpful map.

**But if you're at L1 or L2, you're not building agents. You're building chatbots.**

- 🧠 No memory or persistent state
- ⚠️ No control flow
- 🪄 Just prompt chaining masquerading as autonomy

**At xpander.ai, we don't build copilots. We build L4-L7 autonomous agents, designed for systems, not demos.**

- Stateful execution across real workflows
- Multi-agent orchestration with fallback and delegation
- Governed autonomy with our Agent Graph execution engine

This isn't about playing with AI. It's about deploying it to reduce cost, increase speed, and drive revenue.

**Autonomy without control is chaos.**

We don't ship chaos. We ship infrastructure.
