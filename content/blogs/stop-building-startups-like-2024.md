**STOP building startups like it's 2024.** The game has changed.

**Enterprise-ready** means endless model choices for admins, self-hosted everything, building for the lowest common denominator, predictable costs with predictable mediocrity, and making lawyers happy.

**Enterprise-best** means picking the best models without admin interference, multi-tenant cloud unless we're talking $5M+ deals, building for customers who actually get it, consumption pricing for maximum impact, and making CEOs and developers genuinely excited.

Stop building for procurement. Start building for real autonomous transformation.
