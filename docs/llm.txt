# David Twizer

> Personal site of David (Dudu) Twizer, Co-Founder & CEO of xpander.ai. Essays on AI agents and agent infrastructure, product engineering deep-dives, and homelab write-ups. Before xpander: ~7 years at AWS, most recently leading GenAI Specialist Solutions Architecture across EMEA.

David builds agent infrastructure that runs inside your own environment — your permissions, your APIs, your constraints — so agents can own real, multi-step workflows for hours or days, not just answer questions in a chat box. His bet: every role is becoming a human+AI role, and the companies that learn to onboard, govern, and manage AI employees alongside people will win the next decade.

Site: https://dudutwizer.com — static Hugo site; the "Chat with my AI" button is a live xpander agent that answers questions about David and can help book a meeting.
